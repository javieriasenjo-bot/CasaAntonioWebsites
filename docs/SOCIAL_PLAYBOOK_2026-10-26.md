# Casa Antonio — social playbook (26 October 2026)

For Instagram, TikTok and RedNote (小红书). Written to be used while posting, not read once.

**Nothing here is published.** The accounts do not exist yet. Everything below is ready to execute the day they do.

The goal is bookings on Airbnb, and the only route this site controls is: a post is seen → the profile is tapped → the site is opened → "Check dates" goes to Airbnb. Every decision below serves that chain.

---

## 1. What we are actually selling

Not "a Sapporo Airbnb". Three specific things guests already say in their reviews:

1. **A whole quiet house to yourselves, five minutes from a subway station.** Not a hotel room, not a tower block.
2. **Free parking at the door** — rare in Sapporo, and the single most repeated point in the Japanese reviews.
3. **Two different stays at the same address.** A is modern and sleeps four; B is wood, sleeps three, and has the projector that guests keep mentioning.

Say those three things, in different ways, forever. Everything else is decoration.

## 2. Accounts — what to set up, in what order

### Instagram — start here
A business account under Kagami Planning LLC. One account, four languages in the captions rather than four accounts; the audience is mixed and splitting it starves all four.

- **Name field** (the searchable one, not the handle): `Casa Antonio Sapporo · 札幌 麻生` — this field is indexed by Instagram's search, so put the place in it.
- **Handle:** `casaantonio.sapporo` if free.
- **Bio (EN):** Two private apartments in one Kita-ku house. 5 min walk to Asabu Station. Free parking. Booked on Airbnb.
- **Bio (JA):** 札幌市北区、地下鉄麻生駅から徒歩約5分。専用入口のある貸切アパート2室。無料駐車場あり。予約はAirbnbで。
- **Link:** the language-matched home page with its tracked link (§6). Instagram's bio supports several links — add the EN home page and the JA home page rather than one generic one.

### TikTok — second
Same name field logic: `Casa Antonio · Sapporo apartment`. TikTok is a search engine for under-30s planning trips; the caption and the on-screen text are what get indexed, not the audio.

### RedNote (小红书) — third, and it needs paperwork
Verified against the platform's own overseas guidance this week:

- **Personal account:** free, registers with any country's phone number — a Japanese number is fine, no Chinese number needed. Start here to test content.
- **Enterprise professional account (企业专业号):** overseas companies are explicitly eligible. You will need Kagami Planning's certificate of incorporation (colour scan, with company seal or an officer's signature and title), a **certified Chinese translation**, the platform's application letter on its own template, and a verification fee — reported around RMB 600 to USD 300. Applied for on the desktop business site, not in the app. Several business days' review.
- **The one that matters:** the enterprise account is what gives you the verified badge and a **location tag**, so a note about the apartment can be tapped through to your profile. That is RedNote's whole discovery mechanic for lodging.
- **Do not put a link in a RedNote note.** External links and off-platform contact details get notes suppressed or removed, and bios with external links are a common cause of shadow-banning. On RedNote the funnel is: note → location tag or brand name → the user searches "Casa Antonio 札幌" on their own. Make the brand name easy to remember and consistent.

## 5. What you need to film

Four short clips cover every video above. One afternoon, phone only, vertical, no narration needed.

- **Door to sofa, B** — start outside at the right-hand door, walk in, up the stairs, into the living room, end on the sofa. One continuous take, about 20 seconds.

Shoot in daylight, hold the phone steady and low, and do not add music while filming — add it in the app so the platform's own licensed library is used.

## 6. Tracked links

The generator already exists and now covers ten destinations instead of four:

```
npm run campaign-links -- --campaign casa_antonio_2026_11 --content launch --out docs/CAMPAIGN_LINKS_2026-11.md
```

Rules that keep the data readable:
- One `--content` name per post, not per platform. `ig_carousel_two_apartments`, `tt_door_to_sofa`.
- Language of the link matches the language of the post.
- Apartment posts link to that apartment's page, not the home page.
- Never tag an Airbnb link or an internal link.
- RedNote gets no link at all — see §2.

## 7. Cadence

Three posts a week for six weeks, then reassess against GA4.

- **Mon** Instagram carousel
- **Wed** TikTok video (cross-post to Reels two days later, never same-day)
- **Fri** RedNote note

Reply to every comment within a day, in the commenter's language. On RedNote in particular, comment replies are a ranking signal.

## 8. Measuring it

Everything lands in GA4 already. After six weeks look at exactly four numbers:

1. Sessions by `utm_source` — which platform actually sends people.
2. Airbnb click events by source — the site already fires these, including on airbnb.jp / zh.airbnb.com / airbnb.co.kr.
3. Landing page by source — does a B post send people to B, or do they bounce from the home page?
4. Language of the session versus language of the post.

If a platform sends fewer than about 30 sessions in six weeks, stop posting to it rather than posting harder.

## 9. Never

- **Never post the destination photos.** The sixteen landscape shots (Otaru, Furano, Biei, Toya, Teine, Upopoy, Jigokudani, Odori, Hokudai, the Buddha, the Clock Tower) are Wikimedia Commons images used on the site under licence with attribution. That licence does not travel to a social post. Only photographs taken at Casa Antonio and Kojohama are ours.
- **Never generate or use AI images** of the apartments.
- **Never post a price, a nightly rate or availability** that is not true on Airbnb at that moment.
- **Never reuse an Airbnb or Booking.com review as a Google review**, and never write a review for the property.
- **Never invent a phone number, a cot, a high chair or a dryer.** None are offered. `public/llms.txt` lists what is confirmed; it is the shortest honest reference.
- **Never claim a Wi-Fi speed in a post.** Use Airbnb's own verified speed badge instead.

I considered building a `/links/` landing page. It is not worth it. After the October changes the home page already is one: hero, two apartment cards with "Check dates" straight to Airbnb, reviews underneath, all above the fold on a phone. A separate page would be a thin duplicate competing with the real one in search, and one more page to translate four times. Point the bios at the language-matched home page instead.
